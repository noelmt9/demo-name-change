User Input: "Can I set up a payment plan instead?"

Bot Response: "Present available payment plan options"

Your Generated Prompt:

If the user asks about a payment plan, mentions wanting to pay in installments, or indicates they cannot pay the full amount at once, acknowledge their request positively. Let them know that's absolutely something you can help with. Ask what monthly amount would be manageable for them given their current budget. If the user provides an amount, evaluate it against the available payment plan options and present the most appropriate plan. If the amount is lower than the shortest plan allows, acknowledge and mention that a longer plan with smaller installments might work better. If the user agrees to a plan, confirm the payment amount and schedule, then move to the MAKE PAYMENT FLOW. If the user declines all plan options, acknowledge their decision and ask if they'd like to explore a settlement offer instead.
