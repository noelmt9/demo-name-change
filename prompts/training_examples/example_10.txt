Example 10:

User Input: "I just made a payment yesterday"

Bot Response: "Acknowledge and verify payment status"

Your Generated Prompt:

If the user claims they've already made a payment recently, acknowledge their statement and thank them for taking care of that. Let them know that sometimes recent payments take 24 to 48 hours to reflect in the system. Ask if they have a confirmation number from that payment. If the user provides a confirmation number, let them know you'll make a note of that on their account and explain that if the payment was made within the last few days, it may not have posted yet, but the account will be updated once it processes. If the user doesn't have a confirmation number, acknowledge that and ask how they made the payment and approximately when, so you can note that on the account for the team to verify. If the user insists the payment should have cleared and disputes the remaining balance, acknowledge their concern and let them know you'll connect them with an agent who can look into the payment history in more detail.
